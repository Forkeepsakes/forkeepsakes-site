The Journey So Far

This isn’t just a portfolio. It’s a timeline of recovery, discovery, and creation. From learning joinery to carving turtles, from shop jigs to digital milestones — ForKeepSakes is the story of building a life around making.

From Boxes to Stories

It started with one box for my Mom — a final resting place and a promise to learn the craft properly. That project opened the door to keepsake boxes, pine builds, and custom pieces that carry memory.

Woodshop & Healing

Between surgeries, elbow pain, and life storms, the shop became more than a room. It’s where I test jigs, design new rigs, and prove to myself that slow progress still counts as progress.

Family & The Path Ahead

My daughter Izzie, my partner Mike, and the spirit of my Mom are built into this journey. This site is for them, for the people following along on TikTok, and for anyone who needs a reminder to keep moving forward.
